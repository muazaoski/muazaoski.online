## Persistent style bible

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Unfrog cafe game UI icon, single square transparent PNG. Style bible: chunky rounded low-poly toy 3D, matte mint green #91c9a3, dark forest green #17382e, warm butter gold #f3cf69, cream #f4efdf; restrained coral accent. Orthographic three-quarter view, soft light from upper left, clean bevels and readable silhouette, no thin lines, no platform, no surrounding scene, no text, no badge, no watermark, no harsh black outline. Subject centered with 15 percent transparent padding, fills 70 percent canvas, designed to read at 32px. Consistent cheerful cozy frog-cafe style.
